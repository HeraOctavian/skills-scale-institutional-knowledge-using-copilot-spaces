# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management processes and role guidance. OctoAcme starts by validating a project's business need, success measures, stakeholders, and initial risks. Approved work is turned into a prioritized, estimated plan with clear acceptance criteria and dependencies. Teams deliver iteratively, track progress and risks, and use testing and reviews to maintain quality. Releases follow pre-deployment checks, verification, and rollback planning. Retrospectives turn delivery and incident learnings into owned improvement actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, timelines, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; Developers build and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
