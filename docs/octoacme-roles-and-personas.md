# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide executive-level direction, resources, and decisions for the project's business case and strategic outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Champion the project and confirm alignment with organizational priorities
- Secure or escalate resource needs and resolve decisions beyond the delivery team's authority
- Review progress against intended business outcomes and address major changes in direction

### Collaboration
- Partner with Product Managers on strategic outcomes and significant priority trade-offs
- Receive concise status, risks, and decision requests from Project Managers
- Rely on Developers and other delivery leads for recommendations on feasibility, not day-to-day implementation direction

---

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide technical design and coordinate engineering decisions so solutions are feasible, maintainable, and aligned with project needs.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design, implementation sequencing, and engineering estimates
- Identify technical dependencies and risks and recommend mitigations
- Support code quality, engineering practices, and resolution of complex technical issues

### Collaboration
- Work with Developers on design, implementation, and technical reviews
- Advise Product Managers on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Coordinate with Project Managers on dependencies, estimates, and delivery risks
- Partner with Security / Privacy Leads and Release / Operations Leads on secure and operable designs

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads make sure product workflows and interfaces address user needs and are usable and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create and validate interaction and visual designs
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations and design dependencies

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers to connect user needs to product goals and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with Developers and QA / Test Leads to confirm designs are feasible and testable
- Work with Project Managers to surface design reviews, dependencies, and risks in the delivery plan

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads coordinate the quality strategy and validation evidence for project deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Define test coverage and quality activities based on project risks and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate validation of key workflows, integrations, and regression-sensitive areas
- Report quality gaps and readiness risks and recommend follow-up

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers to make acceptance criteria clear and testable
- Partner with Developers throughout implementation to identify and resolve defects
- Coordinate test timing and readiness reporting with Project Managers
- Work with Release / Operations Leads to confirm critical smoke tests are ready for deployment

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads advise on security, privacy, and compliance requirements and help teams identify and manage related risks.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Review relevant designs and delivery plans and recommend controls or mitigations
- Coordinate required assessments and escalate unresolved high-impact risks

### Collaboration
- Partner with Developers and Engineering / Technical Leads on secure design and implementation
- Work with Product Managers to understand user, data, and product requirements
- Coordinate risk tracking and escalation with Project Managers
- Advise Release / Operations Leads on security and privacy considerations for deployment and operations

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness and deployment practices so changes can be released, monitored, and recovered safely.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release readiness, deployment activities, and operational handoffs
- Confirm monitoring, support readiness, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Surface operational risks and post-release issues to the delivery team

### Collaboration
- Coordinate release criteria, timing, and stakeholder updates with Project Managers
- Validate technical readiness with Developers and Engineering / Technical Leads
- Work with QA / Test Leads on smoke tests and post-deployment verification
- Align customer-facing readiness with Product Managers and Support / Customer Success Leads

---

## Support / Customer Success Leads

### Role Summary
Support / Customer Success Leads represent customer-facing operations before and after delivery, connecting customer needs and feedback to the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Bring recurring customer questions, support trends, and feedback into planning
- Prepare support and customer success teams for upcoming changes
- Coordinate issue triage and route post-release feedback to the appropriate owners

### Collaboration
- Partner with Product Managers to inform priorities, product decisions, and success measures
- Coordinate readiness information and stakeholder communications with Project Managers
- Work with Developers and QA / Test Leads to clarify and triage reported issues
- Align support preparation and customer communication with Release / Operations Leads

---

## Applying Roles
- Assign roles according to project size, risk, and complexity; one person may hold more than one role when appropriate.
- Keep decision ownership explicit when roles are combined, and involve the relevant specialists for security, quality, user experience, and operational risks.

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
